# New sources for robotics company financials and outlooks (found 2026-11-11)

| Source | URL | What it gives | Cost | Machine-readable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEC EDGAR XBRL companyfacts | https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK##########.json | Every tagged revenue/net income by period with accession numbers, for all US filers (Tesla, ISRG, Teradyne, Symbotic, Moog, AeroVironment, Ouster, Serve, Richtech, Agility). Use a descriptive User-Agent; 2s gaps worked. Beware: Tesla/Ouster/ISRG facts repeat across later 10-Qs; filter by `end` and `form`. | Free | Yes (JSON) |
| SEC EDGAR submissions API | https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK##########.json | Filing list with item codes (8-K 2.02 = earnings) and primary document names, which gives the date of each earnings release. Exhibit 99.1 filenames are not listed; read the 8-K's own page for them. `www.sec.gov/.../index.json` returned empty to curl, so use the exhibit name from the 8-K text. | Free | Yes (JSON) |
| Frankfurter (ECB reference rates) | https://api.frankfurter.dev/v1/2026-06-30?base=USD&symbols=CNY,JPY,EUR,KRW,CHF,HKD | Dated USD cross rates for any past day, to convert period-end revenue. Used for all non-USD rows. Note the record's CNY figures use ~7.25, while the ECB rate in mid-2026 is ~6.7-6.8. | Free, no key | Yes (JSON) |
| FANUC IR PDFs | https://www.fanuc.co.jp/en/ir/announce/pdf/2026/financialresult202609_e.pdf (pattern: financialresult + YYYYMM + _e.pdf) | Full tanshin in English with division sales (ROBOT, FA, ROBOMACHINE) and the revised forecast table. Plain PDF text, extracts cleanly with pypdf. | Free | Semi (PDF text) |
| Yaskawa IR PDFs | https://www.yaskawa-global.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/20261009_en.pdf (pattern: YYYYMMDD_en.pdf; deck is YYYYMMDD_haifu_en.pdf) | IFRS half-year results with Robotics segment revenue and operating profit, plus forecast revision table. | Free | Semi (PDF text) |
| ABB interim report PDF | library.e.abb.com ABB-Q3-2026-press-release-English.pdf (link on https://www.abb.com/global/en/news/139696/q3-2026-results) | Group KPIs, outlook, and Robotics divestment footnotes. The abb.com HTML page times out for scripted fetches; the library PDF downloads fine. | Free | Semi (PDF text) |
| HKEXnews filings | https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/YYYY/MMDD/<id>.pdf | UBTech and Dobot interim announcements, in English. Document ids are not guessable; search results surfaced them. The 2026/0824 link found for Dobot was a logo file, so check the title. | Free | Semi (PDF text) |
| pdf.dfcfw.com (Eastmoney PDF mirror) | https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H2_AN<date><id>_1.pdf | Mirror of HKEX/SSE/SZSE announcements, including full Dobot interim report; useful when HKEX is awkward. | Free | Semi (PDF text) |
| Quartr event pages | https://quartr.com/events/<company>-q3-2026_<id> | Fast per-quarter summaries for non-US listings (Midea, UBTech, Fanuc, Yaskawa) with revenue, net income and guidance lines; secondary, so verify against the filing. | Free summaries; paid transcripts/API | Partly (HTML) |
| A3 robot orders | https://www.automate.org/robotics/market-analysis | North American robot orders by quarter. Not queried this pass; recommended for industry demand context next to company results. | Free summaries | Partly |
| JARA (Japan Robot Association) | https://www.jara.jp/en/ | Quarterly Japanese robot orders and shipments; context for FANUC/Yaskawa. Not queried this pass. | Free | Partly (tables/PDF) |
| IFR World Robotics | https://ifr.org/worldrobotics | Annual installations and density by country; already used elsewhere on the site. | Free summary, paid full report | PDF |
| China NBS industrial robot output | https://data.stats.gov.cn | Monthly industrial robot production (units). Not queried this pass. | Free | Yes (CSV/JSON via portal) |
| SSE / cninfo | https://www.sse.com.cn, https://www.cninfo.com.cn | Primary filings for Unitree (688836), Siasun (300024), EFORT (688165), Midea (000333). Not reachable this pass; Chinese-language secondary press was used and flagged. | Free | PDF |
