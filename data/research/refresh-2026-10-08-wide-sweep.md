## 1. Mecka AI raises $60M Series B led by Sequoia to build robot training-data layer

**Category:** Funding (new company, not yet tracked)
**Confidence:** High

Mecka AI, founded 2024, pays people to record themselves doing everyday tasks (making coffee,
fixing cars) while wearing body sensors and using smartphones, then sells the resulting human-motion
data to robotics companies for training humanoid and other robots — explicitly pitched as a
"Scale AI for robotics." The $60M Series B was led by Sequoia with participation from Nvidia and
Microsoft's M12, valuing the company at roughly $500M.

- Date: 2026-10-07
- Sources: [TechCrunch — Robot data startup Mecka AI nabs $60M from Sequoia](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/07/robot-data-startup-mecka-ai-nabs-60m-from-sequoia/)
- Note: some secondary aggregators (pulse2, aibusiness) describe an earlier, separate $60M raised
  in two tranches (a $25M Series A + $35M follow-on) led by Framework Ventures — that appears to be
  a distinct, slightly earlier financing history for the same company. The Sequoia-led $60M/$500M
  figure is the one TechCrunch reports as news on 2026-10-07; flag this for the curator to
  reconcile against Mecka AI's full cap table if it's added as a new company record.

## 2. Minerva Humanoids emerges from stealth with $10M pre-seed (General Catalyst) — humanoid for hazardous jobs

**Category:** Company (new) / Funding
**Confidence:** High

New company. Minerva Humanoids built "Roger," a semi-autonomous humanoid robot remotely steered via
VR headset by a trained human specialist (the robot's own AI, "Minerva Intelligence," handles
balance, navigation and fall recovery). Target use cases: offshore oil & gas, explosive ordnance
disposal (EOD), hazardous material response, airport security. Founders: CEO Sandor Felber
(ex-MIT CSAIL humanoid robotics research, worked on Tesla's Optimus program) and CTO Maurice Rahme
(six years at Boston Dynamics leading the Stretch robot from prototype to commercial deployment).
Round led by General Catalyst, co-led by Long Journey Ventures and Credo Ventures, with ~12 other
participants (MVP Ventures, Compound, Hugging Face, Codecademy founders, etc.) — built in under six
months per General Catalyst's Robin Dechant.

- Date: 2026-10-06
- Sources: [GlobeNewswire — Minerva Humanoids emerges from stealth with $10M pre-seed round led by General Catalyst](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/06/3375277/0/en/minerva-humanoids-emerges-from-stealth-with-10m-pre-seed-round-led-by-general-catalyst-to-build-humanoid-robots-for-the-world-s-most-dangerous-jobs.html) (primary), [TheNextWeb — Minerva Humanoids raises $10M for robots that do bomb disposal and rig work](https://thenextweb.com/news/minerva-humanoids-10m-pre-seed-general-catalyst)

## 3. Safeworld emerges from stealth with $12M+ seed to stress-test gen-AI robots in simulation before deployment

**Category:** Company (new) / Funding
**Confidence:** High

New company, a Carnegie Mellon spinout. Safeworld runs customer robot control software inside
digital twins (built on Genesis / MuJoCo), injecting simulated humans that vary in size, shape,
height, skin color and behavior (tripping, crouching, running, etc.) to find safety failures before
a robot ever meets a real person. Founders: Dr. Ding Zhao (directs CMU's Safe AI Lab), Kyle Wong
(ex-Pixlee co-founder, former Stanford StartX CEO), Simo Rachidi (ML engineer). Round led by Shine
Capital and a16z Speedrun, with Box Group, CMU Endowment, Innovation Endeavors, SV Angel. Early
customer Gritt Robotics (solar-panel installation robots) is already using the platform.

- Date: 2026-10-05
- Sources: [TechCrunch — Can Safeworld convince people that GenAI robots won't hurt them?](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/can-safeworld-convince-people-that-gen-ai-robots-wont-hurt-them/) (primary), [aiweekly.co — Safeworld raises $12M to stress-test gen-AI robots in simulation](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/safeworld-raises-12m-to-stress-test-gen-ai-robots-in-simulation)
