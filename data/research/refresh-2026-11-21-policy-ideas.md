## 2. Pending legislation and executive actions

- Autonomous Warfare Command (not tracked). CRS Insight IN12757, "Autonomous Warfare Command: Background and Considerations for Congress", dated 2026-11-18 (read the PDF). Hegseth's 2026-09-30 memo directs Project Agincourt to create AUTOWARCOM, "a 4-star functional combatant command that will lead the Joint Force in unmanned and autonomous warfare", target 2027-10-01. The Senate FY2027 NDAA (S. 4784, section 917) would instead authorize a Robotic and Autonomous Systems Command (RASCOM) with contracting authority capped at $20 million per contract. The House-passed H.R. 8800 has no comparable provision. S. 4784 has sat on the Senate floor since a 7/27 motion to proceed (per Legis1 summary of the CRS text). Open question for the site: whether to carry a "who controls autonomy inside DoD" thread alongside the 1260H and Covered List threads. In sweep.json.
- Procurement as decoupling (not tracked). The Army's first Manufacturing Rodeo solicitation (2026-11-16) asks for 400 one-way attack drones under $3,000 each by March 1 using no foreign components, with Defense Contract Management Agency audits and teardown inspections, and passing vendors can unlock options for up to 10,000 drones at the March Drone Dominance Gauntlet (SOFX, read). Red Cat missing the Gauntlet II finalist cut and pulling its 2026 sales target (DroneXL, read) is the commercial side of the same program. In sweep.json.
- FAA Part 108 (routine BVLOS) final rule: sent to White House review in July 2026 and still unpublished as of 2026-11-19 (AUVSI, read). My Federal Register search for 'unmanned aircraft' (documents from 2026-11-14) showed no final rule. Not tracked in news.json or policies.json.
- OSTP and OMB FY2028 R&D priorities memo (2026-07-21) names robotics alongside AI, quantum, fusion, space and semiconductors, and directed agency implementation plans within 90 days. A CRS note dated 2026-11-18 says OSTP's national-security science strategy mandate sunsets 2027-08-09 (Legis1 summary, read). Candidate for a policies.json entry if the site wants the "robotics is a named federal R&D priority" fact.
- Compute policy spillover: Trump said on 2026-11-20 (Friday) that tech firms building AI data centers overseas would face "substantial" penalties, with no structure given and administration officials not briefed (Interesting Engineering, read). Reuters headline 2026-11-19: Trump says he and Xi want "moderate AI rules" (headline only; body blocked). Senate Banking hearings with Huang (Dec 3) and Lutnick (Dec 10) are tracked.

## 3. Litigation and agency process

- DJI v. FCC (not tracked; the only DJI record is a Feb petition for reconsideration). DJI filed a mandamus petition in the Ninth Circuit on 2026-11-02 asking it to force the FCC to rule on DJI's Jan 21 Covered List petition; the FCC and DOJ answered on 11-09 that the petition shows DJI's February lawsuit must be dismissed because DJI needs a final order from the full Commission. DJI told the court in April that 25 unreleased products and $1.56B of 2026 sales were stuck (DroneXL weekly, read). Why it matters: the Covered List is the live US lever against Chinese robots and drones, and this is the first court test of how long the FCC can sit on a petition. https://dronexl.co/2026/11/20/dji-asks-court-force-fcc-ruling-brinc-liveops-iphone-skyebrowse-realistic-3d/
