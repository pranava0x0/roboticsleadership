The older 470 value is 2023 on an earlier basis; this is not a comparable decline. [IFR density](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/robot-density-surges-in-europe-asia-and-americas).
- The 2025 country installation values remain about 38,400 US and 354,200 China, supported directly by the country release. [IFR US release](https://ifr.org/downloads/press_docs/EN-2026-SEP-24-IFR_Press_Release_WR-USA.pdf).
- IFR's approximately 7,000 full-size professional humanoids excludes some uses and is distinct from Counterpoint's broader roughly 16,000 installations. Neither is silently added to industrial robot counts. [IFR service-robot definitions](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/global-sales-of-professional-service-robots-surge-24-percent).
- Cone Drive states it manufactures harmonic gearing in Michigan; the site's zero-US-reducer assertion was false. Automotive lidar and EV-cell shares are explicitly proxies, not measured robot-component shares. [Cone Drive](https://conedrive.com/harmonic-gearing-ring-series/).
- 1X capacity/preorders are not deliveries; Agility's two additional GXO sites are planned for early 2027; Skild ARR is not recognized annual revenue; Gecko's IDIQ ceiling is not obligated spending. These corrections appear in the company records with their sources.
- NSF sunset NRI 3.0 in 2022. ARPA-E CATALCHEM-E selected projects; an announced selection does not mean applications remain open. [NSF notice](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/information/dcl-nri-sunset-announcement/nsf22-081), [ARPA-E selections](https://arpa-e.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/CATALCHEM-E_selection_project%20descriptions_final_1.pdf).
- Treasury's October 26 outbound-investment rules are proposed; FCC lab restrictions are draft. Coverage is scoped to the regulated transactions/devices rather than all robotics. [Treasury proposal](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-26/pdf/2026-21786.pdf), [FCC draft](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-425721A1.pdf).
- DOE's four laboratory project selections are subject to award negotiations and later funding. NASA OSAM-2 concluded before an orbital demonstration. [DOE selections](https://www.energy.gov/science/articles/doe-announces-four-national-laboratory-led-selections-advance-robotics-and), [NASA OSAM-2](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/on-orbit-servicing-assembly-and-manufacturing-2-osam-2/).
