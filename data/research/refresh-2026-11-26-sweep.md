### 1. USTR finds China's excess capacity actionable under Section 301 and proposes tariffs; robotics is a named sector with its own report chapter
- Event date: 2026-11-25 (USTR press release; FR notice docket USTR-2026-0278 / -0741)
- Agency: USTR (14 economies; China, EU, Japan, Korea, Taiwan, India, Vietnam, etc.)
- Summary: USTR determined China's acts relating to structural excess capacity are unreasonable and actionable; the burden finding lists robotics among 22 sectors (aluminum ... robotics, semiconductors, ships ...). Proposed action for China: tariffs up to 20% net of the forced-labor 301 rate on goods not in the annexes, and up to 10% on Annex A goods, with Annex B exemptions drawn from the US-China Board of Trade "30-for-30" lists; it accounts for the 20% rate in the Kuala Lumpur Joint Arrangement. Proposed rates elsewhere: Vietnam up to 35%, India up to 25%, Singapore 20%, EU/Japan/Korea/Taiwan/Switzerland up to 15%. Mexico and Norway: no determination yet. Written comments and requests to appear due 2027-01-05; hearings 2027-01-25 at the ITC.
- Robotics chapter (China report, Sector Annex, "XIV. Robotics", about PDF p. 373-377): China installed 354,000 industrial robots in 2025 vs 38,000 in the US and 208,000 rest of world; 2020-2025 installation CAGR China 15.0%, US 4.2%, RoW 2.6%; Chinese-headquartered suppliers' share of China installs rose from 18% (12,420 units, 2015) to 53% (187,620 units, 2025); cites the 14th five-year robotics plan, the 15th FYP naming robotics a strategic emerging industry, and the 2025 NDRC state VC guidance fund (~$138B over ten years).
- Primary: https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural
  - China FR notice PDF: https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/files/Press/Releases/2026/China%20FRN.pdf
  - China report PDF: https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/files/Press/Releases/2026/SECP%20301%20China%20report.pdf
- Secondary: Bing/Google News listings (feedstuffs, steelorbis, Yahoo Finance on Vietnam 35%)
- Read primary: Yes (press release, FRN text and the China report robotics chapter via PDF extraction). Not yet in Federal Register proper at time of check (pre-publication PDF; FR API showed nothing).
