    How to read this

    On the measures tracked here, the US leads on AI and capital, and China on robot installations and the supply chain. The site tracks those numbers. It does not predict which lead matters more.

    • US leads: frontier AI, humanoid startups and venture capital.
    • China leads: 59% of world robot installations in 2025, more than half of global cobot shipments, and 2 million robots in operation.
    • Contested: components, batteries, sensors, and how fast either lead spreads.
