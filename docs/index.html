    How to read this

    What this is

    On the measures tracked here, the US leads on AI and capital, and China on robot installations and the supply chain. The site tracks those numbers. It does not predict which lead matters more.

    1. Where America leads, on the measures tracked here — frontier AI, humanoid startups, and venture capital.
    2. Where China leads — 59% of world robot installations in 2025, more than half of global cobot shipments, and 2 million robots in operation.
    3. What’s contested — components, batteries, sensors, and how fast either lead diffuses.

    Sources for each number are on the US vs China scoreboard. Its “edge” calls are editorial judgments, not scores.
