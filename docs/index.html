Robotics — How the U.S. can Lead

America has the AI. China has the scale. Robotics decides who gets both — this site keeps the score.

Bottom line

Robotics is where America’s AI lead collides with China’s manufacturing lead. Whoever combines intelligence with scale first sets the terms of the physical economy — and neither side has both yet. Funding, deployments, and policy tracked below, every record cited to a primary source.

  1. America leads where robots are invented and funded — frontier AI, humanoid startups, and venture capital.
  2. China leads where robots are built and bought — 54% of world robot installations, more than half of global cobot shipments, and 2 million robots in operation.
  3. Neither lead is safe. AI diffuses fast and deployment compounds. The decisive variable this decade isn’t invention — it’s policy.

Every number above is tracked, with primary sources, on the US vs China scoreboard.
