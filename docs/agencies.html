What U.S. agencies are doing on robotics.

One table, sourced. Where each agency funds robotics, what it deploys, and where to apply or follow.

Agency R&D focus Applications Manufacturing Programs & investment
OSTP Office of Science and Technology Policy (Executive Office of the President) Doesn't fund R&D directly; coordinates federal priorities through the National Science and Technology Council (NSTC) and advises POTUS via PCAST. Cross-cutting AI policy — embodied AI, autonomous systems, safety, workforce — sets agency priorities downstream at NSF / DOE / NIST / NASA. Indirect — shapes CHIPS Act implementation priorities and Manufacturing USA emphases.
  • Winning the AI Race: America's AI Action Plan (July 2025) — 90+ recs across Innovation / Infrastructure / Diplomacy pillars.
  • Director Michael Kratsios (confirmed Mar 2025); Lynne Parker as Counselor + PCAST exec director.
  • Public statements emphasize NIST AI RMF as the federal procurement baseline.
DOE Department of Energy + 17 national labs National-lab robotics + AI-driven materials and manufacturing. ORNL, Argonne, Sandia, INL all run robotics + self-driving-lab programs. Grid inspection, nuclear fuel-cycle handling, AI-enabled defense manufacturing. EERE Advanced Manufacturing Office; ARM Institute (Manufacturing USA, robotics-specific).
  • Exascale Foundry — ORNL + Autonomous Resource Corp, AI-enabled defense manufacturing partnership (May 2026).
  • National-lab LDRD funds self-driving labs across ORNL / Argonne / LBNL (IFP brief).
ARPA-E Advanced Research Projects Agency — Energy (DOE sub-agency) High-risk energy R&D; periodic solicitations for embodied AI / robotics applied to energy systems. Robot-driven grid maintenance, fuel-cycle automation, autonomous wind/solar O&M. Adjacent — manufacturing tooling shows up in some open program-area awards.
  • Embodied AI for Energy Systems open call; typical award $1–10M.
  • FY 2026 request ≈ $478M total; up to 4 new focused solicitations in FY26.
DOC Department of Commerce (parent of NIST, NTIA, EDA) Industrial policy + standards rather than direct R&D. Semiconductor fab automation; regional robotics clusters via Tech Hubs. Central — administers CHIPS funding and designates Regional Tech Hubs.
  • CHIPS and Science Act — $52.7B for semiconductor manufacturing + advanced manufacturing R&D.
  • EDA Regional Tech Hubs — Phase 2 designation includes robotics-anchored clusters.
NIST National Institute of Standards and Technology (within DOC) Engineering Lab: Intelligent Systems Division, robotics performance test protocols. Industrial humanoid performance metrics; safety standards for human-robot shared workspaces. Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP) — small-manufacturer assistance.
  • AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI 100-1) — voluntary; increasingly cited in procurement.
  • Standards-setting role; not a direct grant funder.
NSF National Science Foundation Foundational + academic robotics research across CISE and Engineering Directorates. Co-robots, human-robot interaction, integrative systems, agricultural + healthcare robotics. Through Engineering Directorate and Manufacturing USA partnerships.
  • NRI 3.0 — up to $3M/award; multi-agency (NSF, USDA, NASA, DoT, NIH, NIOSH).
  • Foundational Research in Robotics (FRR) — rolling submissions.
  • FY26 House mark proposes NSF cuts; AI + quantum prioritized.
NASA National Aeronautics and Space Administration JPL robotics — rovers, manipulators, autonomous exploration. Mars rovers (Perseverance), Astrobee (ISS), CLPS lunar landers. In-space assembly + manufacturing via COSMIC industry consortium.
  • OSAM-1 cancelled Feb 2024 after cost growth $0.7B → $2B+.
  • DARPA RSGS — provides expertise; no longer prime.
  • FY24 budget ≈ $24.8B total agency.
Space Force U.S. Space Force (USSF) Autonomous space systems, on-orbit servicing in partnership with AFRL + DARPA. Satellite servicing, refueling, dynamic space operations, in-orbit inspection. Procures; doesn't manufacture. Drives commercial robotic-satellite-service market.
  • 4 servicing missions launching 2026: DARPA RSGS, Astroscale refueler, Tetra-5 (with AFRL), Otter.
  • Otter contract — $54.5M to Starfish Space.
  • FY26 SAML budget ≈ $9.7M (down 66% from $29.2M).
DARPA Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DoD) High-risk autonomy research; technology-transition to services + commercial primes. Autonomous aerial / ground / maritime systems; satellite servicing. Funds prototype manufacturing through cooperative agreements.
  • RSGS — Mission Robotic Vehicle launches 2026 via Space Logistics; NRL-built arm.
  • Replicator initiative (DoD-wide, DIU-led) — $1B Tranche 1 + Tranche 2 expanding to ground.

Cross-cutting: The most consequential federal robotics money in 2026 sits at the intersection of DoD Replicator (procurement at scale), CHIPS Act (manufacturing), and 100% bonus depreciation (demand-side). The R&D agencies (ARPA-E, NSF, NASA JPL) generate the technology; DoD + Commerce buy and scale it.
