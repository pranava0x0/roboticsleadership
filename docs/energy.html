Robotics for Energy.

Where robots and autonomy are already at work across the energy system — from boiler walls and live transmission lines to solar fields, turbine blades, geothermal rigs, mines, data centers, and orbit. Curated, with a primary source on every entry.

Bottom line

The AI build-out has made energy the hottest robotics market nobody is tracking. Inspection robots are already standard at power plants and substations; autonomy is moving up the stack into building solar farms, repairing turbine blades, drilling geothermal wells, mining the critical minerals, and even assembling power in space. The same nexus driving the dataset — compute demand, reindustrialization, and physical AI — runs straight through the grid.
