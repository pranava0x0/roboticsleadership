State robotics policy.

Where robots are actually allowed to operate is decided in statehouses and city councils, not just Congress. This is the lay of the land: the key themes, what's already on the books, and where to read further.

Bottom line

The states are the rulebook. 20+ states authorize sidewalk delivery robots, nearly all regulate autonomous-vehicle testing, and a competitive thicket of tax credits courts robotics jobs — while state AI laws creep toward autonomous physical systems and a federal preemption fight looms. There is no single national framework, so the map is the policy.
